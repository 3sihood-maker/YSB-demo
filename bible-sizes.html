How long is a cubit?

A cubit is elbow to fingertip. It was never fixed like a metre, so every Bible size here changes a little depending on which one you pick.

Background

18 inches is the figure most Bibles and study notes use. The royal cubit (a cubit and a handbreadth) is the one Ezekiel describes. Anything marked est. is a best guess, not a number the Bible gives.
