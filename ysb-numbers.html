Printed or digital?

We don't think it's one or the other. Young people need both. Here are the numbers behind each.

The big picture

A huge generation, and a hungry one

2bn+

Gen Alpha (born 2010–2024). The largest generation the world has ever seen.

49%

of Gen Z adults in the US now read the Bible weekly. Up from 30% a year earlier!

78%

of Christian teens read or listen to Scripture weekly in Kenya. In France it's 8%. Gen Alpha isn't the same everywhere.

99%+

of the world's people now have some Scripture in their own language. 544 languages are still waiting.

Print

A Bible you can hold

Something to carry, mark, keep and give away.

  1. 19million

    Bibles sold in the US in 2025

    That's a 21-year high, and double the number sold in 2019. The UK is seeing it too, with Bible sales up 106% since 2019.

    US · UK
  2. 69%

    of Bible users read a printed Bible

    In a typical month, compared with 48% who used a Bible app. Even among adults under 58, print still came out on top (69% to 53%).

    US · 2022
  3. 54studies

    say paper helps us understand

    Across 54 studies and more than 171,000 readers, people understood more when they read on paper than on a screen. And that gap has been growing.

    Global research
  4. 148.3million

    printed Scriptures shared in 2025

    Bible Societies gave out 22.4 million full Bibles, 10 million New Testaments and nearly 116 million Portions last year. Paper still carries the Word a long way.

    Worldwide
  5. 74%

    of teens online 6+ hours a day feel lonely

    That's in Kenya, compared with 57% of teens online less than three hours a day (a link, not proof of cause). A printed Bible has no notifications. It's a quiet place to meet with God.

    "I have a Bible app, but I feel like most of my faith things are in person." – US teen
    Kenya · US
Digital

A Bible in their pocket

Meeting young people where they already are, every day.

  1. 4.7billion

    people use mobile internet

    That's 58% of the world, on a phone or device of their own. The Bible can be right there with them.

    Worldwide
  2. 82%

    of 15–24 year olds are online

    Young people are the most connected age group on the planet (72% for everyone else). In the poorest countries they're almost twice as likely to be online as older people.

    Worldwide
  3. 1billion

    Bible App installs

    YouVersion passed one billion device installs in 2025. That's a lot of pockets with a Bible in them!

    Worldwide
  4. 32.2billion

    Bible chapters viewed on screens in 2025

    Up 13.8% in just one year, and audio Scripture was played 1.4 billion times. People are opening the Bible digitally all over the world.

    Worldwide
  5. 1 in 3

    teens learn about faith mostly online

    And 1 in 4 say their faith has changed because of something they learned or someone they met online. Among the most engaged Christian teens, 61% have recently used a Bible app. Digital space can be sacred space.

    US · ages 13–17

Hold it. Carry it.
