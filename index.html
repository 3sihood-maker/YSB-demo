Two Connected Pilots for the Global Strategy Team — November 2026

There is a new and exciting pilot we would like to introduce to the GST.

The foundation of this pilot dates back to 1987 and a ground-breaking sports movement project that is still multiplying to this day.

… and it was pink.

1987

This was the world’s first Sports Bible. Printed in English and Spanish for the Pan American Games in Indianapolis.

A simple idea.

Yet an idea that had never been done in the 500 years of the Bible being printed.

This powerful pink Bible broke new ground and made the way for every sports Bible since … in many languages, for many sports and often catalyzed by a major sports event.

The Bible has always been central to the sports movement. Now the sports movement is seeing sports-themed Bibles across the world.
